ISSUE 1 — CRITICAL: Mistral-7B prompt template never shown
==============================================================
LOCATION: Section 5.3 "User-side Semantic Representation"

PROBLEM:
  The paper says Mistral-7B generates "2-sentence summaries"
  of user interests, and this is described as the paper's
  primary generative AI contribution. But the exact prompt
  used is never stated.

  The actual prompt (from generation_config.yaml) is:
    "You are analyzing a short-video platform user. Based on
     the following videos they watched most, write a 2-sentence
     summary of their interests in plain English. Be concise.
     Videos: {raw_profile}
     Summary:"

  Without this prompt, no one can reproduce B1-Gen or B2-Gen.
